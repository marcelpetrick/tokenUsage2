This shows a breakdown of September 2026: the models used, their providers and
tokens consumed, plus an estimate of what the same usage would have cost via
the APIs. Most of it was covered by subscriptions. The mix includes leading
hosted models and self-hosted #Qwen-based #Ollama runs. Because assigning an API
price to self-hosted inference would be misleading, those rows show token usage
without an invented dollar amount.
